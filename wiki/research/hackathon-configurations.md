# What the hackathon credential configurations look like today

> **Note (2026-10-21).** The Issuer carries the two hackathon types, Developer Passport and Achievement, as VCDM 2.0 configurations on roster predicates (`on_roster`; `on_roster` then `winner`), not the chain below. This note is the inventory they were migrated from and is otherwise unchanged.

Research note, recorded 2026-10-20. Decided the same day, after this inventory: the chain is not carried into the Issuer; the VPID is dropped, and Developer Passport and Achievement are issued on two roster predicates (on the roster; `winner`), so the presentation, anchor and validity rows of the migration table below reduce to those two checks (`wiki/project/issuer-v0.1/responsibilities.md`, sections 5 and 7). Inventory of the three hackathon credential types (VPID, Developer Passport, Achievement Certificate) as they exist in `mosip-asia/ait-vc` on branch `playground/transcript-demo`, read only, for the work of absorbing them into the Issuer as VCDM 2.0 configurations with Bitstring status lists, issued by Certify behind a Gateway checklist. Nothing here has been applied; the `ait-vc` repository is frozen and untouched. Paths below are relative to `D:\MOSIP\ait-vc\local-stack\` unless they name another repository.

## Short answer

All three types are Certify 0.14 `credential_config` rows in `certify_init.sql`, each with its own base64 Velocity template, its own scope, its own JSON-LD context file under `config/` served by the `certify-nginx` container as `http://certify-nginx/<name>-context.json`, and the same signing key, DID, data-provider plugin and identity table. They are VCDM 1.1 end to end: `https://www.w3.org/2018/credentials/v1`, `issuanceDate` and `expirationDate`, no `credentialStatus`, and a `credential_status_purpose` of `revocation` that Certify ignores on a v1 context. The VPID is a root credential claimed by plain OpenID4VCI against the mock eSignet, whose token `sub` is the identity row id. The Passport and the Achievement are issued only after a Node issuance page runs an OpenID4VP request for one `VPIDCredential`, sends the presentation to a Node policy service (Inji Verify signature and expiry, VCGA trusted-issuer list, activation, chain), looks the VPID up in an organiser roster, writes the anchor, team and validity columns into the identity row, and then sends the wallet to claim through the normal OpenID4VCI flow. Nothing of this exists in `deployment/`, `docs/`, `ait-web-wallet/` or any `playground/` folder; it lives only in `local-stack/`.

## Shared and different

Shared by all three:

- One Certify, one issuer display ("AIT University Registrar"), one `did:key` DID refreshed at bring-up, one Ed25519 key (`CERTIFY_VC_SIGN_ED25519`), one suite (`Ed25519Signature2020`), one binding method (`did:jwk`), one `id` prefix (`https://ait.example/credential/`). `config/certify-csvdp-student.properties` lines 16–19, 37–42; `certify_init.sql` lines 399–403, 449–453, 499–503.
- One data-provider plugin (`PostgresDataProviderPlugin`), one table (`certify.identity_data`), one seed CSV, one row per person that all three types template from, and one scope-query shape (`select * … where id = :id`). `config/certify-csvdp-student.properties` lines 24–35; `certify_init.sql` lines 100–172.
- One vocabulary prefix, `ait: https://ait.example/credential#`, repeated in each context file, so `vpid`, `anchorId` and `teamId` expand to the same IRIs whichever credential carries them. `config/vpid-context.json` line 5, `config/passport-context.json` line 5, `config/achievement-context.json` line 5.
- One context-serving mechanism (static files on `certify-nginx`) and one in-stack server for the standard contexts (`context-server` aliasing `www.w3.org` and `w3id.org`, already serving `/ns/credentials/v2`). `docker-compose.yaml` lines 52–60; `contexts-nginx.conf` lines 29–49; `docker-compose.mock-esignet.yaml` lines 127–142.
- One authorization server (mock eSignet), one wallet client (`wallet-demo`), the same `sub` meaning (identity row id). `docker-compose.mock-esignet.yaml` lines 186–189; `mock-esignet/README.md`.
- One VCGA entry permitting all three types for the one DID; one policy service; one issuance page for the two anchored types, parameterised by a `CREDENTIAL_TYPES` map. `config/vcga-trusted-issuers.mock.json`; `policy-service/server.js`; `issuance-page/server.js` lines 63–84.
- The same VCDM 1.1 envelope in every template and the same inert `ARRAY['revocation']` status purpose.

Different per type:

- Three `credential_config` rows, three scopes, three base64 templates (no switch inside a template), three context files, three display blocks and colours (`#1e293b`, `#0f766e`, `#7c3aed`). `certify_init.sql` lines 382–521.
- Expiry source: `vpidValidUntil` (seeded), `passportValidUntil` and `achievementValidUntil` (computed by the page as the earlier of the closing date and the anchor's expiry). `certify_init.sql` lines 428–429, 478–479; `issuance-page/server.js` lines 180–207.
- The VPID is a root with no presentation requirement and no page; the Passport and Achievement require one `VPIDCredential`; the Achievement additionally requires the roster's `winner` flag. `issuance-page/server.js` lines 63–84, 260–264.
- Sign-in branding: scope `vpid_vc_ldp` is presented as "Sandbox Citizen Registry"; the others fall under the wallet's default sign-in. `mock-esignet/server.js` lines 128–131.
- Wallet exposure: the Mimoto entry that would show the VPID is disabled, and the enabled entry's static well-known lists neither the Passport nor the Achievement. `config/mimoto-issuers-config.mock.json` lines 27, 54; `config/issuers/ait-registrar/.well-known/openid-credential-issuer`.

## What a 2.0 migration changes

The transcript has already been through this in the Issuer's own stack; `wiki/concepts/vc-revocation.md` lines 42–64 records the three changes and the decoded `ait-transcript` template shows the result (`vc-stack/certify_init.sql` lines 150–170). Applied to the three hackathon rows:

| VCDM 1.1 trait today | Where | What 2.0 requires |
|---|---|---|
| `context` column ends in `https://www.w3.org/2018/credentials/v1` and the template's `@context` lists the same | `certify_init.sql` lines 396, 446, 496 and the three templates | Both become `https://www.w3.org/ns/credentials/v2`, and they must agree exactly because Certify compares the wallet's requested context list with the stored one by size and content (`vc-revocation.md` line 50). The transcript row already does this: `vc-stack/certify_init.sql` line 159 and the template's `@context` |
| `issuanceDate: ${validFrom}` and `expirationDate: ${<type>ValidUntil}` | the three templates | Rename to `validFrom` and `validUntil` (`vc-revocation.md` line 51). The value sources can stay: the transcript uses Certify's `${validUntil}`, the hackathon rows read a per-row column, and the page's earlier-of rule only needs the column name to survive |
| No `credentialStatus` block | the three templates | Add the dot-notation block (`id`, `type`, `statusPurpose`, `statusListIndex`, `statusListCredential` from `${credentialStatus.*}`), not a bare `${credentialStatus}` which Velocity renders as Java map syntax (`vc-revocation.md` lines 52–64) |
| `credential_status_purpose = ARRAY['revocation']` present but inert | `certify_init.sql` lines 415, 465, 515 | Becomes live once the context is v2: Certify 0.14 calls the status-list service only then (`vc-revocation.md` line 44; the repository's own note at `certify_init.sql` lines 524–525). `ledger-enabled=true` and the allowed purpose are already set (`config/certify-default.properties` lines 203–208) |
| Status list signed with `Ed25519Signature2020` | `config/certify-csvdp-student.properties` line 25 | Already the value the Issuer's transcript stack uses (`vc-stack/config/certify-csvdp-student.properties` line 21); the `BitstringStatusListCredential` Certify signs is v2 only (`contexts-nginx.conf` lines 33–35) |
| Standard v2 context must resolve | `contexts-nginx.conf` lines 36–38 | Already vendored and served in-stack as `/ns/credentials/v2`; nothing to add for the hackathon types |
| Credential `credentialSubject.id = ${_holderId}` (did:jwk), credential `id` from the `id-field-prefix-uri` | templates; `config/certify-csvdp-student.properties` line 19 | Unchanged by 2.0, but the chain check compares `anchorId` to the VPID's `id` (`policy-service/server.js` line 173), so the Issuer's `id` convention must stay stable across the migration |
| Type-specific context files | `config/*-context.json` | Nothing in them names a VCDM version; they define terms at the top level, the shape the Issuer chose for the transcript in v0.1 (`wiki/research/signing-coverage.md`, decision line), so they carry over as they are once their host moves from `http://certify-nginx/` to the VDR |
| Policy service verifies with `skipStatusChecks: true` | `policy-service/server.js` line 61 | Once the VPID carries a status-list entry, a Gateway "required presentation" check has a revocation signal the hackathon never had; the choice to honour it is new |

## Hackathon decisions in `D:\MOSIP\docs\adr\` that touch these types

- 0001 (accepted, 2026-09-07) JSON-LD only, no selective disclosure, trust assumed between parties: all three are `ldp_vc` with `Ed25519Signature2020`, and an unproven VP is accepted at the issuance page.
- 0002 (accepted, 2026-09-07) A credential chain is formed by presenting the anchor at issuance, and the issuer defines the anchor: the Passport and Achievement record `anchorId` and never outlive it (the earlier-of validity rule).
- 0003 (accepted, 2026-09-10) Anchored credentials are issued by a Track 4 issuance page that chains OID4VP and OID4VCI, not by Certify's native interactive authorization: the reason the issuance page exists instead of Certify's `/oauth/iar`.
- 0004 (accepted, 2026-09-10) The VCGA trusted-issuer check lives in one policy service in front of Inji Verify: the `trusted-issuer` check and the static JSON list.
- 0005 (accepted, 2026-09-10) Wallet activation is a presentation-time rule, separate from the credential chain: the `activation` check (a valid VPID for the same holder key) versus the `chain` check.
- 0006 (proposed, 2026-09-12) An issuance requirement is a presentation definition over a named credential group, and the trust framework limits which groups an issuer may require: the generalisation of the page's single `VPIDCredential` input descriptor; not implemented in `local-stack/`.
- 0007 (proposed, 2026-09-12) A root credential is a role the trust framework assigns per scope, not a credential type, and a wallet may hold none or several: the VPID keeps its name as the sandbox's national-scope root; the note that participants were not issued a VPID in the phase-2 demo.
- 0008 (proposed, 2026-09-12) A credential is primary or derived according to whether it carries an anchor; both are valid, and a derived credential goes inert with its anchor: the Passport and Achievement as derived credentials whose `anchorId` is the VPID's `id`.
